About Roots Magma Unisex Salon

Amravati's Trusted Legacy in Hair & Beauty Since 1985

Established in 1985, Roots Magma Unisex Salon (located at Next Level Mall, Ground Floor Shop No. 5, opposite Hotel Great Mehfil, Camp Road, Amravati) is one of Amravati's most loved hair & skin destinations. For nearly four decades, we have provided precision grooming, advanced hair therapies, and wedding day artistry for both men and women.

Our senior stylists continually train in the latest international techniques, offering unhurried, personalized attention with premium dermatologically-tested products.
